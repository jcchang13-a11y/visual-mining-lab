ABOUT / 這個計畫在幹嘛
人話：我們想試試看，能不能不要把 AI 做成一個「你問一句、它答一句」的工具，而是慢慢養出一個會自己消化東西的東西。現在它暫時長了五個器官：有的負責讀、有的挖材料、有的出去找證據、有的拆問題、有的負責消化和分配。重點不是五個器官本身，而是它們能不能真的互相影響，能不能吃進原本不會的東西，遇到垃圾會吐掉，遇到矛盾會卡住而不是亂講，甚至有一天發現自己的身體不夠用時，能在受控測試下長出新的結構。這個東西叫 ZENOMORPH。它現在還不是完成品，更不是生命；這個網站就是我們看著它施工、失敗、變形的地方。
AI 話：ZENOMORPH is an experimental morphogenetic / metabolic cognitive architecture whose system boundary is defined by stable participation in a traceable cognitive metabolism rather than by model, repository, process, or tool boundaries. Its current five-organ morphology—SHROOMING, MU/TH/UR, DROPLET, GUT, and VAJRA—is treated as a provisional developmental state. The engineering objective is to test whether heterogeneous external materials and capabilities can be ingested, evaluated, decomposed, routed, recombined, and eventually assimilated such that they produce persistent, provenance-preserving changes in subsequent system behavior. Candidate structural changes are not granted unrestricted self-modification: incorporation follows isolated construction, adversarial and cross-organ validation, provenance closure, rollbackability, and regression testing. The long-term research question is whether a bounded agentic architecture can progress from modular orchestration toward controlled cognitive morphogenesis without confusing tool access, repeated execution, or superficial plugin accumulation with genuine assimilation.
NAME / 命名
ZENOMORPH 是自造詞：ZEN + MORPH。它改寫自 Xenomorph——xeno- 源自希臘文 xénos，意為陌生、外來、異鄉；-morph 源自 morphē，意為形態、形狀，因此 Xenomorph 字面上可理解為「異來之形／外來形態」，後來因《Alien》系列而成為「異形」最熟悉的稱呼。ZENOMORPH 把 XENO 換成 ZEN：ZEN 取「禪」的聲音與概念——不把任何暫時形成的答案、分類或形態當成最後真理；MORPH 仍指形態與變形。它保留 Xenomorph 的語音暗影，但指向一個可以攝取、拆解、消化、同化外來材料與能力，並因此改變自身結構與行為的人工認知有機體。NOSTROMO 則保留為它目前的 habitat / incubation architecture，也就是棲地、孵化器與工程平台。
