# ZENOMORPH Dream Phase — Shadow Protocol v0.1

Status: CANDIDATE / SHADOW ONLY
Stable mutation: forbidden
External-feed acquisition during dream: forbidden

## Purpose
Test whether offline recombination of already-ingested evidence can improve retention, cross-food transfer, or produce persistent non-predeclared wiring without contaminating Stable.

## Evidence rule
DISPLAYED STATE MUST FOLLOW EVIDENCE. Every generated episode MUST carry `identity=DREAM/SYNTHETIC` and provenance links to its source fragments. Synthetic material MUST NOT be relabelled observed/live, and MUST NOT enter Stable directly.

## Four-arm isolated experiment
A. replay: provenance-preserving replay of prior ingested fragments.
B. perturbed replay: replay with controlled ordering/noise perturbation.
C. counterfactual recombination: combine fragments across foods, failure traces, rejected candidates, and organ residues without adding new external facts.
D. no-dream control: identical evaluation window without offline recombination.

## Wiring
DROPLET: closed to new acquisition during dream; may expose only previously admitted immutable references.
MUTHER: read -> decompose -> recombine -> mutate; generated structures remain synthetic.
GUT: assembles isolated episodes and records source-fragment graph.
VAJRA: attacks provenance leakage, observation/simulation identity collapse, memorization, and false transfer.
SHROOMING: may retain only candidate wiring that survives post-wake gates; no Stable control.

## Gates
candidate -> isolated generation/assembly -> stress/provenance -> cross-organ -> regression -> held-out -> cross-food -> delayed validation -> incorporation decision.

Required comparisons: pre-sleep vs immediate post-wake vs delayed retest; dream arms vs no-dream control. Test catastrophic forgetting as well as apparent gain. A gain with provenance corruption is failure.

## First run input pool
Use only existing ingestion artifacts and their provenance: heterogeneous prior food fragments, failure logs, rejected candidates, and organ residues. Do not preselect material because it appears semantically suitable for dreaming. Do not use or modify Rongzhe source documents directly.

## Promotion rule
No Dream Phase mechanism, episode, or wiring is Stable merely because it is novel or interesting. Promotion requires reproducible benefit on unrelated held-out/cross-food material, delayed persistence, regression safety, and intact provenance/identity boundaries.
